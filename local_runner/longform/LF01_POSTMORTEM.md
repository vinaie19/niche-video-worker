# LF01 "How Big Is the Universe? (If Earth Were 1 cm)": retention post-mortem (31 Oct 2026)

Data: YouTube Studio retention curve at about 1 day, roughly 47 views and 1,134 impressions (93%
from Suggested, CTR 2.35%; Browse CTR 8.3%). The sample is small, so read the shape, not the
decimals.

## What the curve shows (mapped against the script timeline)

| Time | Retention | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04 | 100% -> ~60% | Big Earth shrinking to a tiny marble on black | "If Earth were a marble, one centimetre wide, everything we've ever launched..." |
| 0:04-0:08 | ~60-66% (small bump) | Tiny Earth + ruler; Milky Way flash behind | "...would fit inside a small town." |
| 0:08-0:20 | 60% -> ~35% | Tiny marble on black, small grey text, small teaser thumbnails | "But build the rest... won't fit on Earth... Every size, every distance... Let's walk it." |
| 0:20-0:35 | ~35% -> ~8% | Two dots (Earth, Moon) on a thin ruler on black | "Let's start close to home. The Moon becomes a tiny grain..." |
| 0:35-2:00 | ~8% (a couple of rewatches) | Sun, football pitch, planets | - |
| after 2:00 | 0% | Milky Way onwards (never reached) | - |

Nobody reached the best payoffs (Voyager, Andromeda, 73 light-years). The video was decided in
the first 35 seconds.

## Why viewers left, ranked

1. **The picture (biggest cause).** From about 0:05 to 0:40 the frame is almost entirely black
   with tiny objects: a 1 cm marble, a thin ruler and two dots. This is the same failure as ep23
   (162 views) and the zoom v1. True-scale accuracy made the frame empty. Viewers arriving from
   Suggested, after a thumbnail with a galaxy on it, got a black screen.
2. **The setup was too long before the first payoff.** 20 seconds of premise ("rules of the
   model") before anything happens; the first concrete payoff ("the Moon is 30 cm away") lands at
   0:26-0:30. Long-form hooks on Suggested traffic have about 5-10 seconds.
3. **Audience mismatch.** 93% of impressions were cold Suggested traffic next to established
   long-form science channels (polished, music, a real presenter or 3D). Our channel's viewers
   are Shorts viewers. A first long-form on a Shorts channel gets this traffic.
4. **The voice: contributing, not primary.** The Andrew voice is fine, but it was a lone voice
   over silence plus a faint hum, with no music and no sound design. Over a black screen, that
   reads as "slideshow". It amplifies cause 1 rather than causing it.

**"People feeling bad about the video":** there's no evidence of this. Average view duration is
short, but there's no sign of negative sentiment (no dislike or comment data indicating it). The
data shows boredom/unmet expectation in the first 30 seconds, not dislike.

## Rules for LF02 and every long-form after it

- Seconds 0-5: open on the single most spectacular full-frame image in the video (the best
  payoff), with motion, then promise: "by the end you'll see X". No rules or setup first.
- No frame may be mostly black. Every step is shown big and full-frame (as in the Shorts zoom
  v2); true-scale diagrams appear only as a 2-3 second insert, never as the main picture.
- The first concrete payoff by 0:10; a new reveal every 15-20 seconds after that.
- Music bed (royalty-free, low) plus a whoosh or hit on every transition; never voice over
  silence.
- The thumbnail is a photoreal single subject (AI image, as in YouTube's suggestion), 2-3 words,
  never the answer.
- Launch long-form only together with 2-3 feeder Shorts that link to it (Related video), so its
  first viewers are warm, not cold Suggested traffic.
