**Conclusions (rules from now on)**
1. Frame 0 = one big, bright, real object filling the frame (planet, star, Sun). No diagrams, no black with small dots, no text-only openers.
2. Every video has a **human-scale anchor** in the picture: a person silhouette, a football pitch, a city, a coin, your kitchen.
3. Default format = misconception: a thing you believe, shown; then the real thing, shown; the "why" is the last line.
4. Title = curiosity gap or contradiction statement, under 50 characters, no numbers unless the number is the shock.
5. 18-24 s, cut on the last word, last frame rhymes with the first (loop).
6. Thumbnail (and frame 0, which Shorts show in the feed) = the object + 2-4 huge words.

## 3. Next 8 Shorts (all $0, real NASA/ESA imagery, laptop build)

Each: title · frame 0 · hook (spoken) · human anchor · payoff (last line) · thumbnail words

1. **A Day on Venus Is Longer Than Its Year**
   Frame 0: full-frame Venus (Magellan/Mariner 10 PD image), slowly turning backwards.
   Hook: "On Venus you'd have a birthday before the Sun set."
   Anchor: a person standing on the surface watching the Sun crawl.
   Payoff: "Venus goes round the Sun faster than it turns. Its day is longer than its year."
   Thumb: VENUS / "ONE DAY = 243 DAYS"

2. **Saturn's Rings Are Thinner Than a House**
   Frame 0: Cassini rings edge-on filling the frame, then tilting.
   Hook: "These rings are wider than 20 Earths side by side."
   Anchor: a two-storey house next to the ring's edge.
   Payoff: "Most of it is about ten metres thick. Scaled to a sheet of paper, the sheet would be a kilometre wide."
   Thumb: SATURN'S RINGS / "10 METRES THICK"

3. **You're Seeing the Sun 8 Minutes Ago**
   Frame 0: the real white Sun from orbit, flaring.
   Hook: "If the Sun switched off right now, nothing would change. For eight minutes."
   Anchor: a kitchen clock, a window, sunlight on a hand.
   Payoff: "You never see the Sun. You see where it was eight minutes ago."
   Thumb: THE SUN / "YOU SEE THE PAST"

4. **Olympus Mons Is Too Big to See**
   Frame 0: real Mars globe (Viking/MOLA), dive to the volcano.
   Hook: "Stand on the biggest volcano in the solar system and you can't see it."
   Anchor: Everest dropped beside it (3x), then France-sized footprint.
   Payoff: "It's so wide its slope hides the peak behind the horizon."
   Thumb: MARS / "A VOLCANO THE SIZE OF FRANCE"

5. **The Moon Is Leaving Us**
   Frame 0: full-frame Moon (LRO), slowly drifting back.
   Hook: "The Moon is 4 cm farther away than it was last year."
   Anchor: your fingernail growth = the same speed.
   Payoff: "It's stealing Earth's spin. A dinosaur's day was 23 hours. Yours is 24. And it's still getting longer."
   Thumb: THE MOON / "IS DRIFTING AWAY"

6. **Mercury Is Not the Hottest Planet**
   Frame 0: Mercury (MESSENGER) baking, cut to Venus.
   Hook: "The planet closest to the Sun is not the hottest."
   Anchor: a pizza oven (Mercury day) vs a blast furnace that never cools (Venus).
   Payoff: "Mercury has no blanket. Venus has one 90 times thicker than ours. Venus wins, day and night."
   Thumb: MERCURY / "NOT THE HOTTEST"

7. **Betelgeuse Might Already Be Gone**
   Frame 0: Orion photo, push into Betelgeuse (Hubble/ALMA image).
   Hook: "This star might not exist anymore."
   Anchor: the light you see left when the Romans ruled Britain (640 years).
   Payoff: "When it blows, it'll be visible in daylight. And it may already have happened."
   Thumb: BETELGEUSE / "ALREADY DEAD?"

8. **Uranus Rolls Around the Sun**
   Frame 0: Uranus (Voyager 2 / Hubble) rotating on its side.
   Hook: "One planet is lying down."
   Anchor: a ball rolling along the floor vs a spinning top.
   Payoff: "Something hit it hard enough to tip the whole planet. Its poles get 42 years of daylight, then 42 of night."
   Thumb: URANUS / "ROLLS ON ITS SIDE"

Feeders for the long video (cut-downs, ~20 s each, Related video = the long one): Voyager "50 years, barely left the driveway"; "Every star you can see is in one galaxy"; "Andromeda's light left before humans existed".
