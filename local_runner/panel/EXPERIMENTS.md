## Proven style to keep in the mix (owner, 5 Nov 2026)

Not a fixed rule. The Crab Nebula flight preview (ep30 shot 1) is a proven style worth repeating: rich natural colour, a glowing full-screen subject, and smooth 3D flight movement from frame 1.
- Keep making this kind regularly, but keep exploring new formats alongside it (real footage, size comparisons, timely events, new ideas). Each new format is logged here as its own experiment.
- Whatever the format, the quality bar holds: beautiful, colourful, moving. Nothing soft, grainy, flat or dull.

### How the flight style is built (reference for when we use it)

The owner called the Crab flight preview "too good": rich colours and nice movement.

**The look**
- A gorgeous, colour-rich real image as the world we fly into: Webb, Hubble, ESO, NASA. Big (4,000-10,000 px), so it
  stays sharp when we zoom to 1:1.
- The subject starts small in deep space and grows to fill the screen: an approach, not a static frame.
- A 3D star-field flight on top (longform/starflight.py): near stars stream past with streaks, far ones barely move.
  The parallax is what makes it feel like travel. It slows and stops on arrival.
- One continuous camera move, no cuts, crossfading between real images of the same object at deeper scales.
- Bold two-line hook text from frame 1 (white line plus amber line); captions synced to the voice; a ring on the real
  target when we arrive.
- The payoff is the real thing: the real photo or the real object, never an artist's impression.

**What we avoid** (each has failed before)
- Static photos, flat zooms into one image, soft or upscaled shots, dim or low-contrast images (Io infrared, X-ray-only
  footage), shots carried by a caveat.
