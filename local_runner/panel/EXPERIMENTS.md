## Visual style rule (owner, 5 Nov 2026)

The benchmark is the Crab Nebula flight preview (ep30 shot 1): rich natural colour, a full-screen glowing subject, and smooth flying movement with depth (a parallax star field) from frame 1. Every new Short must look this beautiful.
- Vary the topics: no repeats of the same object or story. Keep the look: colourful real imagery (nebulae, galaxies, planets, aurora, eruptions) plus a moving 3D flight.
- Reject on sight: soft or grainy footage (for example X-ray time-lapses or tiny volcano plumes), flat zooms into one photo, static shots, and dull grey or black frames.
