# Market study: what space Shorts break out (4 Nov 2026)

Method: 34 topic searches found 90 space/physics channels; their Shorts tabs gave 3,660 Shorts (1,515 space
Shorts from 50 channels after removing off-topic ones and tiny channels). Each Short is scored as
views / that channel's median Short views, so a high score means the idea worked, not the channel's size.
"Breakout" = top 10% (>= 3.9x the channel median). Full output: market_report.txt; data: market_shorts.csv.
Scripts: market_scan.py, market_analyse.py.

Examples of breakouts: "Flight from the Earth to the Milky Way Black Hole" (ESO, 509x, 5.6M),
"Solar System Planet Size Comparison 3D" (4.8M), "How Small Is DNA?" (12M), "The Largest Eruption on Earth
Ever Caught on Camera" (5.4M), "Saturn's Rings Disappearing" (3.4M, timely), NASA eclipse 2024 (15M).

## Agrees with our own channel
Our #1 Short is a zoom journey (Zoom Out, 2,305 views, 57.6% stayed); extreme objects stay best (~54%).

## Rules for Gate 0 (topic) and Gate 2 (packaging)
1. Format: prefer a journey/zoom (Earth -> extreme object, or object -> unimaginable scale), or real footage
   of something extreme, or a timely event. Plain explainers are average; "visualized/3D" is below average.
2. Topic: extreme objects or scale. Avoid alien/mystery and gentle solar-system trivia.
3. Title: a superlative and/or a concrete number. No question-mark titles, no caps, no hashtags (#shorts only).
Caveats: title keywords only approximate the format; 27-57 Shorts in the top formats; correlation, not proof.
